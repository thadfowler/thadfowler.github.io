## Novel—*The Taste of Fruit Not Eaten*

In fiction, the extraordinary is within grasp, and my intention as a creative person is to seize it. Why limit our experience to the world as we see it when so much more is possible? <a href="/docs/imaginative/novel/" style="color:#111827;"><em>The Taste of Fruit Not Eaten</em></a> is my most developed imaginative expression to that end and more. It's forthcoming from Running Wild Press, and I'd be delighted if you check it out.

## Technical communication

My aim as a technical writer is to enable people to achieve tangible goals that are important to them.

People rarely read a piece of technical writing for fun, but when they complete a pressing task thanks to it, they see immediate, material gain. I want to give people that experience and, by doing so, drop a little positivity into the world.

## My background
For those interested in more about me, here are a few tidbits:

#### Personal

- I was born in New Orleans and graduated from high school there. As my family also moved around a fair amount during that span of time, migration played a significant role in my early life.
- As an adult, I lived in San Francisco for over two decades, and I currently live in the Washington, D.C. area. I treasure experiences from every place I've lived.
- I grew up reading—books, newspapers, anything. Inspired, I tried out writing and relished how text made observable artifacts of my mind's workings. I was hooked. Text assumed a role as engine in my life, and it has propelled me over the long haul.

#### Professional

- My educational grounding is broad:
    - In-depth study spanned international relations, economics, finance, foreign languages (French, Spanish, Japanese), literature, semiotics and other text-focused theory, creative writing, and pedagogy.
    - My curiousity took me into additional areas like the visual arts and math as well. 
- I taught college writing and literature early in my career.
- I pivoted into work as a content specialist, editor, and manager within an instructional design/development team. In one way or another technical writing has factored into that work throughout and has only grown as an interest over time. 
- Further details about my experience are available on my <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/thad-fowler-3b4a3825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LinkedIn profile</a>.
