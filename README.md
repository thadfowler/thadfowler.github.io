# Thad Fowler—Writer and Editor

A writing portfolio site built with Hugo (Docsy theme). It showcases imaginative fiction and technical writing examples, includes a news section, and provides ways to connect.

## Site Structure

### Landing page
Introduces visitors to the portfolio, linking to key sections.

### About page
Shares my background as a writer.

### Writing section
A hub for my written work, split into two subsections:

- **Imaginative**
  - Curated fiction entries that link out to where the writing appears (third-party sites).

- **Technical**
  - Curated technical entries that link out to where the writing appears (PDFs and/or third-party sites).

### News section
A blog-style feed where I share new work and other updates.

### Connect page
Links to my social media.

## Content Linking
The writing examples are presented via outbound links to:

- PDF documents
- third-party websites where my work appears

## Where Site Content Lives (Hugo)
Site content is located in:

- content/en
